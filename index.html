Sorted fewest-cards-first by default — “Missing” rows are real-world members (rosters researched Aug 2026) with zero cards in the game, then come the members who haven't gotten much love. Duo and ship cards count for both members. Code, Like and Hidden exclusives count when the card name identifies the member (“100K GISELLE” credits Giselle); Daily and Wheel cards always count. A member who left their group and now has cards as a solo act or in another group shows as “Solo” / “Now in …” rather than missing. Click a member to see every card they're on — or where a missing member already has cards under another group. A “missing” member can also just be a spelling difference between the roster and your card name.
